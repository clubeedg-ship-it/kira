# OttoGen Website Redesign Brief — March 8, 2026

## THE PROBLEM WITH CURRENT SITE
Too many services listed (website, hosting, email, support, AI, rebranding, consultancy, cost saving, digital management). Buyers can't understand the core product in seconds.

## THE NEW POSITIONING

### One Core Offer
**Your outsourced digital & AI team — one subscription, one point of contact, everything handled.**

### ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
Small businesses, 2-20 employees, no internal tech team. Currently paying €200-800/month across 3-5 separate digital providers (hosting, web agency, random IT support, no AI strategy). Frustrated, overpaying, unsupported.

### What We Sell (Outcomes, NOT Activities)
- ✅ Less confusion — one team instead of five providers
- ✅ Fewer suppliers to manage
- ✅ Faster support when something breaks
- ✅ Someone competent steering your digital & AI direction
- ✅ Cost control — often saves money vs. current multi-provider setup
- ✅ Peace of mind — it's handled

### DO NOT use the word "replace" anywhere.
