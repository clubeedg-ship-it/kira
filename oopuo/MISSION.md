## The Product (Phase 1)

**One core offer:** Outsourced digital & AI team for small businesses (2-20 employees) without an internal tech team.

**Why it works:**
- SMB owners juggle 3-5 separate digital providers
- They become the middleman between their own suppliers
- Nobody's looking at the full picture or steering AI direction
- They overpay, get slow support, and waste hours coordinating

**The promise:** One team. One conversation. Everything handled.

**Pricing:**
| Tier | Price | Scope |
|------|-------|-------|
| Essentials | €300/mo | Website, hosting, email, DNS, 5h support, basic AI, quarterly review, 48h response |
| Growth | €500/mo | + 12h support, full AI automation, monthly review, 24h priority, dedicated contact, cost audit |
| Custom | Variable | Larger/specific needs |

**Competitive edge:** A freelancer does tasks. An agency does projects. We run your digital operation.
