## Usage Notes

- **Personalize the {{variables}}** — always reference their company, a recent post, or something specific
- **Send connection request only** — don't include a message if the platform limits it; save the pitch for follow-up
- **Follow up 2–3 days** after they accept — not immediately
- **Track responses** in a simple spreadsheet: Name | Persona | Template Used | Response | Next Step
- **Never send more than 20 connection requests/day** to avoid LinkedIn restrictions
- **A/B test**: Try different templates for the same persona and track which gets more replies
