# OttoGen → Oopuo Personal Brand: Social Media Strategy

*Created: 2026-03-13*

---

## 1. Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone
- **Confident, not arrogant.** Otto is 20 and building a portfolio company — let the work speak, don't oversell credentials.
- **Technical but accessible.** Explain AI in terms SMB owners understand. No jargon walls.
- **Future-forward with grounded proof.** Vision posts anchored by real results and client wins.
- **Swiss Cyberpunk aesthetic.** Clean, precise, slightly dark. Think Zurich meets Blade Runner — minimal design, neon accents, monospace type.

### Vocabulary
- Use: "build," "ship," "automate," "scale," "portfolio," "infrastructure"
- Use: "AI-powered," "workflow," "system," not "solution" or "synergy"
- Avoid: "hustle," "grind," "guru," "hack," "disrupt"
- Never: "serial entrepreneur," "thought leader," "game-changer"

### Do's
- Share real numbers (revenue, time saved, automation stats)
- Show the work — screenshots, dashboards, before/after
- Reference the portfolio naturally (IAM, CuttingEdge, Chimera) as proof points
- Be direct. One idea per post.
- Credit tools and collaborators

### Don'ts
- Don't fake vulnerability for engagement ("I almost gave up...")
- Don't post motivational quotes
- Don't compare to Musk/Zuck/etc.
- Don't use "we" when it's Otto — own the singular voice
- Don't oversell. Understate and overdeliver.

## 2. Platform Strategy

### X/Twitter — Primary (5x/week)
**Why:** Fastest feedback loop. Tech/AI community lives here. Investor eyeballs.
**Content:** Short insights, build-in-public updates, hot takes on AI for SMBs, threads on automation wins.
**Frequency:** 5 posts/week (1 thread, 4 short-form)

### LinkedIn — Secondary (3x/week)
**Why:** SMB decision-makers and investors browse here. Credibility builder.
**Content:** Longer-form case studies, portfolio updates, AI implementation breakdowns, webinar promos.
**Frequency:** 3 posts/week

### YouTube — Growth Channel (1x/week)
**Why:** Evergreen content. Webinars, tutorials, and philosophy pieces compound over time.
**Content:** 10-15 min videos — AI walkthroughs for SMBs, behind-the-scenes of building Oopuo, webinar recordings.
**Frequency:** 1 video/week

### Instagram — Brand Aesthetic (2x/week)
**Why:** Visual brand reinforcement. Swiss Cyberpunk aesthetic lives here.
**Content:** Reels (60s AI tips), carousel breakdowns, workspace/aesthetic shots.
**Frequency:** 2 posts/week (1 reel, 1 carousel or static)

## 3. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: AI for Real Businesses
Practical AI implementations for SMBs. No hype, just results.
- "We automated [client]'s inventory emails — saved 12 hours/week"
- "3 AI tools every interior design firm should use in 2026"
- Webinar clips: live automation builds

### Pillar 2: Building at 20
The raw reality of building a portfolio company young, self-taught, in the Netherlands.
- "I moved to the Netherlands at 18 with nothing. Here's what I'm building now."
- Weekly portfolio updates (revenue, milestones, lessons)
- Honest failures and pivots (OttoGen → Oopuo rebrand story)

### Pillar 3: The Future of Work & AI
Philosophy and vision — where AI, privacy, and business intersect.
- "Why SMBs will outperform enterprises in the AI era"
- Chimera teasers: privacy-preserving AI and why it matters
- Hot takes on AI news filtered through an SMB lens

### Pillar 4: Behind the Portfolio
Showcasing the Oopuo ecosystem — IAM, CuttingEdge, Omiximo, ZenithCred — as proof of the thesis.
- "How one automation system serves 5 different companies"
- Client spotlights and transformation stories
- Cross-company synergies and the portfolio strategy explained
