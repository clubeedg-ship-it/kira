## Visual Guidelines — Swiss Cyberpunk Aesthetic
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif (Helvetica Neue, Inter) with glitch/neon accents
- **Colors:** Matte black backgrounds, electric cyan (#00FFFF), magenta (#FF00FF), Swiss red (#FF0000)
- **Imagery:** Grid layouts, data visualizations with glow effects, circuit-board patterns over Swiss landscapes
- **Carousels:** Dark slides, one key stat per slide, neon highlight on keywords
- **Videos:** Lo-fi tech overlays, terminal-style text animations, subtle scanline effects
