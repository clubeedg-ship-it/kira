## 1. Oopuo — Portfolio Overview & Synergies

### 1.1 Mission & Thesis

Oopuo operates as a venture studio and operational holding company, building and scaling early-stage ventures across EdTech, AI services, corporate wellness, interior design, and consumer goods. Our thesis centers on capital-efficient execution: we provide shared operational infrastructure — finance, legal, marketing systems, and strategic oversight — enabling each portfolio company to move faster with lower burn.

### 1.2 Portfolio Snapshot

| Company | Sector | Stage | Status | Revenue Model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IAM (Interactive Move) | EdTech / Hardware | Pre-revenue / Outreach | 🟢 Active | Hardware sales + SaaS content license |
| OttoGen | AI Services / Consulting | Early revenue | 🟢 Active | Project-based + retainer |
| ZenithCred | Corporate Wellness / SaaS | Seed fundraise | 🟡 Fundraising | B2B SaaS subscription |
| CuttingEdge | Interior Design / PM | Revenue-generating | 🟢 Active | Project management fees |
| Abura Cosmetics | Consumer Goods / Beauty | Revenue-generating | 🟢 Active | Product sales (B2C + wholesale) |

### 1.3 Portfolio Synergies

Oopuo's portfolio is deliberately constructed to share resources and amplify each company's reach:

- **Shared AI Infrastructure:** OttoGen's AI capabilities are leveraged across the portfolio. ZenithCred's gamification engine benefits from OttoGen's LLM integration expertise. IAM's content platform roadmap includes AI-generated interactive modules prototyped through OttoGen's R&D pipeline.
- **Cross-Selling Channels:** CuttingEdge's network of corporate clients in interior design creates warm introductions for ZenithCred's workplace wellness platform. Abura Cosmetics' retail and e-commerce distribution knowledge feeds back into IAM's go-to-market for hardware sales.
- **Centralized Operations:** All five companies share Oopuo's back-office stack — bookkeeping, legal counsel, HR compliance, and CRM tooling — reducing per-company overhead by an estimated 30–40% versus standalone operation.
- **Brand & Content Flywheel:** OttoGen's Swiss cyberpunk aesthetic and content strategy expertise has informed brand guidelines across the portfolio. Abura Cosmetics and IAM both leverage visual identity frameworks originally developed under OttoGen's creative direction.
- **Investor Network:** ZenithCred's active fundraise surfaces investor relationships that benefit the entire portfolio for future rounds. Every pitch meeting is an opportunity to introduce the broader Oopuo thesis.

## 2. IAM (Interactive Move)

**Website:** [interactivemove.nl](https://interactivemove.nl)  
**Sector:** EdTech — Interactive Learning Hardware  
**Stage:** Pre-revenue / Market Entry  
**Geography:** The Netherlands (primary), EU (expansion target)

### 2.1 Executive Summary

IAM develops and sells interactive floor and wall projection systems designed specifically for kindergarten and early childhood education environments. The product transforms physical spaces into immersive learning environments — children interact with projected games, stories, and educational content through body movement.

Q1 2026 is defined by aggressive outreach into the Dutch kindergarten market. The team has been executing a direct outreach strategy targeting kindergartens, childcare chains, and municipal education procurement offices across the Netherlands. Initial demo sessions have generated strong qualitative feedback, with educators highlighting the product's ability to combine physical activity with cognitive learning — a key differentiator in the Dutch early education philosophy of play-based learning.

The hardware is sourced and configured, the content library covers core early learning modules, and the sales pipeline is being built through a combination of cold outreach, referral partnerships, and attendance at regional education fairs.

## 4. ZenithCred

**Sector:** Corporate Wellness / Gamification / SaaS  
**Stage:** Seed Fundraise  
**Fundraise Target:** €1.1M at €2.5–3.5M pre-money valuation  
**Product Status:** MVP demo built

### 4.1 Executive Summary

ZenithCred is building a gamification layer for corporate wellness programs. The platform transforms employee health and wellness initiatives — fitness challenges, mental health check-ins, nutrition tracking, mindfulness — into an engaging, competitive, and rewarding experience using points, leaderboards, team challenges, and redeemable credits.

The MVP demo is complete and has been presented to prospective investors and early design partners. The product demonstrates the core loop: employees earn "Cred" through wellness activities, compete on team and individual leaderboards, and redeem credits for rewards configured by their employer.

The primary Q1 activity is the seed fundraise. The round targets €1.1M at a pre-money valuation of €2.5–3.5M, providing 18–24 months of runway to achieve product-market fit, sign 10–15 enterprise clients, and build toward Series A metrics.
