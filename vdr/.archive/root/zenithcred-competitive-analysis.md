## 2. SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- **Unique hardware moat**: Light panels + IAM interactive projectors create physical engagement no software competitor can replicate
- **Biofeedback integration**: Real-time physiological data feeds gamification — not just step counts
- **Token economy**: More sophisticated than points-and-badges; potential for cross-employer or ecosystem value
- **IAM heritage**: Proven hardware (Interactive Move) with existing installations provides credibility
- **European base**: GDPR-native, positioned for EU corporate wellness growth

### Weaknesses
- **Early stage**: Seed round limits sales capacity and enterprise credibility vs. $3B Personify Health
- **Hardware dependency**: Longer sales cycles, installation complexity, higher CAC than pure SaaS
- **Limited AI/personalization**: Behind Personify Health and Limeade on ML-driven engagement
- **Small team**: Execution risk on simultaneous hardware + software + go-to-market
- **Unproven unit economics**: Hardware + SaaS hybrid model not yet validated at scale

### Opportunities
- **Corporate wellness gamification growing at 13-23% CAGR** — fastest-growing segment within broader wellness market
- **Post-COVID return-to-office**: Companies investing in physical office experiences to attract employees back — perfect fit for interactive panels
- **ESG & employee wellbeing mandates**: EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) drives wellness investment
- **White-label / B2B2B**: Sell through office fit-out companies, co-working spaces, corporate real estate
- **Biofeedback data moat**: Aggregated anonymized wellness data could become valuable for insurers and HR analytics

### Threats
- **Personify Health / Wellable adding hardware partnerships**: Could integrate third-party devices and narrow the gap
- **Economic downturn**: Wellness budgets are discretionary; hardware is first to be cut
- **Commoditization of gamification**: Points, badges, leaderboards are table stakes — differentiation must go deeper
- **Enterprise sales cycles**: 6-18 months for hardware deployment vs. 30-day SaaS trials
- **Copycat risk**: Chinese/Asian manufacturers could clone light panel hardware at lower cost
