## 1. Executive Summary

Employee wellness programmes fail for one reason: nobody uses them. The average corporate wellness app sees **engagement drop below 20% within 90 days**. Companies invest thousands per employee in digital tools that become notification noise.

**ZenithCred takes a fundamentally different approach.** We combine interactive hardware — floor and wall projection systems with real-time biofeedback — with a gamification token economy that makes wellness physical, social, and rewarding. Instead of asking employees to open another app, we transform break rooms, lobbies, and common areas into interactive wellness zones that people *want* to use.

**What makes ZenithCred unique:**

- **Interactive projection hardware** — proprietary light panels and IAM-derived projectors that respond to movement, creating immersive physical experiences
- **Biofeedback integration** — real-time physiological data from wearables feeds into the gamification engine, rewarding actual wellness outcomes, not just step counts
- **Token economy** — employees earn ZenithCred tokens for participation, team challenges, and wellness milestones, redeemable for meaningful rewards
- **Physical space activation** — transforms underutilised office areas into engagement hubs, supporting return-to-office strategies

**Why it matters now:** European companies face mounting pressure from the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) to demonstrate employee wellbeing investments. Simultaneously, organisations are spending billions to make offices worth commuting to. ZenithCred sits at the intersection of both trends.

We are proposing a **[4/6]-week pilot programme** to demonstrate measurable impact on employee engagement, physical activity, and wellness outcomes at [Company Name].

- Advanced challenges and leaderboard finals
- Post-pilot wellness survey administered
- Data analysis and ROI report compiled

## 5. Hardware Requirements & Setup

### Space Requirements
| Requirement | Specification |
|---|---|
| **Floor area** | Minimum 3m × 3m per projector (clear, flat surface) |
| **Ceiling height** | 2.5m – 4.0m |
| **Surface** | Light-coloured floor or wall (no dark carpet) |
| **Power** | 1× standard EU socket (230V) per unit |
| **Network** | Wi-Fi (WPA2) or Ethernet — min. 10 Mbps up/down |
| **Ambient light** | Indoor lighting acceptable; no direct sunlight on projection area |

### Hardware Provided (on loan during pilot)
- **2× IAM Interactive Projector Units** — ceiling-mounted, short-throw projection with motion tracking
- **1× Biofeedback Bridge** — connects wearable data streams to the platform
- **Mounting hardware** — non-invasive ceiling mounts (no structural modifications)

### IT Requirements
| Item | Details |
|---|---|

| Wearable sync (optional) | Bluetooth LE via bridge unit or employee phones |
| SSO integration (optional) | SAML 2.0 / OAuth 2.0 for employee authentication |
| Data residency | EU-hosted (Amsterdam). Fully GDPR-compliant. |
