## 2. IAM Kindergarten Sales Pipeline

### Finding Decision-Makers in Netherlands/Benelux

**The goldmine: Landelijk Register Kinderopvang (LRK)**
- Official Dutch government register of ALL childcare facilities
- URL: https://duo.nl/particulier/landelijk-register-kinderopvang.jsp
- Contains: name, address, type, municipality of every registered kinderopvang
- **~38,000 childcare locations in NL** (kinderdagverblijf, BSO, peuterspeelzaal)
- Public data — can be scraped or downloaded

**How to build your list:**
1. Download/scrape LRK for all "kinderdagverblijf" and "peuterspeelzaal" entries
2. Use Saleshandy or LinkedIn to find directors/managers (locatiemanager, directeur)
3. Enrich with email using Saleshandy/Hunter.io
4. Large chains to target first: KidsFoundation, Partou, Kindergarden, CompaNanny, Smallsteps

**Key decision-makers:**
- **Locatiemanager** (location manager) — for individual locations
- **Regiomanager** (regional manager) — for chains, controls multiple locations
- **Bestuur/Directie** — board/management for independent kindergartens
- **Inkoop/Facility manager** — for large organizations

### Best Sales Channels (ranked)

1. **LinkedIn + warm DMs** (🏆 #1 for NL) — Dutch business culture values relationships. Connect → value message → demo offer
2. **Phone calls** (#2) — Kindergartens answer phones. Direct, personal. Call 10:00-11:30 or 14:00-15:30 (outside pickup/dropoff)
3. **Email** (#3) — General info@ emails often ignored. Need personal emails.
4. **Trade shows** (#4) — Expensive but high-intent:
   - **Kinderopvang Totaal** (annual, NL) — THE event for childcare
   - **BETT Show** (London) — EdTech, international buyers
   - **Didacta** (Germany) — Education trade fair
5. **Partnerships/Distributors** — See below

### Do Kindergartens Buy Direct or Through Distributors?

**Both, but distributors dominate for hardware:**
- EyeClick sells through **reseller network** (AV integrators, educational suppliers)
- WizeFloor sells through **country distributors** (e.g., WizeFloor UK is a separate entity)
- Dutch educational suppliers: **Heutink**, **De Rolf Groep**, **Nienhuis Montessori**
- AV integrators who serve education: **BIS Group**, **Crestron partners**

**Recommendation:** Start with **direct sales** to prove the product, then recruit 2-3 distributors once you have 10+ reference installations.

### Message That Converts

**Subject line:** "Interactief spelen op de vloer — gratis demo voor [naam locatie]"

**Key angles:**
- Children learn through movement + play (backed by Dutch "spelend leren" philosophy)
- Works for all ages, inclusive for children with special needs
- Low maintenance — no toys to clean/replace
- "Wow factor" for parents → competitive advantage for enrollment
- Already used by [X] kinderdagverblijven in NL (social proof)

**CTA:** Free 30-minute live demo at your location (bring the projector, let kids play)

### Competitor Pricing

**EyeClick:**
- Beam (ceiling-mounted): ~€5,000-€8,000 (hardware only)
- Obie (portable, tabletop/wall): ~€2,500-€4,000
- Software subscription: ~€50-100/mo for content library
- Total first year: €6,000-€10,000+

**WizeFloor:**
- WizeFloor One (mobile stand): ~€6,000-€9,000
- WizeFloor Pro (ceiling): ~€8,000-€12,000
- Content creation app included
- No mandatory subscription

**IAM Pricing Strategy:**
- If hardware cost is lower → undercut at €3,000-€5,000 + €49/mo content
- If comparable → match at €5,000-€7,000 but offer **free trial month**
- Consider **lease model**: €199-299/mo all-in (very attractive for kinderopvang budgets)
- **Demo-to-close rate** in EdTech hardware: typically 20-30% when kids interact with product

## 3. Otto AI Services Pipeline

### Highest-Margin, Easiest-to-Deliver Services (Start Here)

1. **AI Chatbot / Customer Support Bot** — €2,000-€5,000 build + €200-500/mo retainer
   - Build with: Voiceflow, Botpress, or custom (OpenAI + your stack)
   - Margin: 80%+ (mostly your time)
   - Delivery: 1-2 weeks

2. **Workflow Automation** (Make.com/n8n/Zapier) — €1,500-€5,000 per workflow
   - Examples: Auto-invoice, lead routing, email parsing, CRM sync
   - Margin: 85%+ 
   - Delivery: 3-7 days per workflow

3. **AI Content Generation System** — €1,000-€3,000 setup + €500/mo retainer
   - Social media, blog posts, email sequences automated
   - Margin: 75%+

4. **AI Sales Outreach Setup** — €2,000-€4,000 (set up their cold email/LinkedIn)
   - You literally set up what this document describes, for clients
   - Margin: 90%

### Pricing Models

| Model | When to Use | Range |
|-------|------------|-------|
| **Project fee** | First engagement, clear scope | €1,500-€10,000 |
| **Monthly retainer** | Ongoing optimization/support | €500-€3,000/mo |
| **Value-based** | When you can quantify savings | 10-20% of annual savings |

**DO NOT sell hourly.** It caps your income and clients hate open-ended billing.

**Starter offer:** "AI Automation Audit" — €500-€1,000 flat fee. Analyze their business, identify 3 automatable processes, deliver a roadmap. This converts to project work 40-60% of the time.

### Lead Generation Pipeline

1. **LinkedIn Content (organic, free)**
   - Post 3-5x/week: AI automation tips, case studies, before/after
   - Format: short text posts, carousels showing workflows, video demos
   - Target: Dutch SMB owners (MKB), operations managers
   - Expected: 1-3 inbound leads/week after 30 days of consistent posting

2. **LinkedIn DMs (warm outreach)**
   - Connect with SMB owners who engage with AI content
   - Message: "Saw you're in [industry]. We helped a similar company automate [X] and save 20 hours/week. Worth a 15-min chat?"
   - Expected: 10-15% reply rate on warm leads

3. **Cold Email (via Instantly)**
   - Target: NL SMBs with 5-50 employees in services (accounting, recruitment, marketing agencies)
   - Use Saleshandy to find them
   - Expected: 1-3% meeting rate on cold email

4. **Referrals**
   - After first 3 clients, ask for referrals. Offer €200-500 referral bonus.
   - This becomes your #1 channel by month 6.

### Competitor Research — Successful AI Agencies

- **Latenode** — workflow automation, from $17/mo SaaS
- **Relevance AI** — AI agent platform for agencies
- **Floik** — AI demos/guides
- **Most successful model:** Solo consultants on LinkedIn selling €2K-€10K automation projects to SMBs. Not agencies — individuals with personal brands.
