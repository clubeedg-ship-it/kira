# IAM — Follow-Up Email Nurture Sequence
> **Target:** Dutch Kindergartens (Kinderdagverblijven) — post initial cold outreach
> **Trigger:** No reply after Template A/B/C cold sequence
> **Language:** Dutch (with English reference translations)
> **Tone:** Professional, warm, no-pressure — consistent with existing IAM templates

---

## Sequence Overview

| Email | Day | Theme | Goal |
|-------|-----|-------|------|
| 1 | Day 3 | Value Add | Give before asking — share useful resource |
| 2 | Day 7 | Social Proof | Build trust with peer validation |
| 3 | Day 14 | Case Study | Show concrete, relatable results |
| 4 | Day 21 | Limited Offer | Create gentle urgency |
| 5 | Day 30 | Last Chance | Final touch — door open, no pressure |
