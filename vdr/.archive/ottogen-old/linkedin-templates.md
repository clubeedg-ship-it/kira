## Usage Notes

- **Always personalize** every [BRACKET] — generic DMs get ignored
- **Send connection request first** with a short note, then DM after accepted
- **Follow up once** after 5-7 days if no reply — don't spam
- **Track responses** in CRM with template ID (1A, 2C, etc.) to measure what converts
- **Best send times:** Tue-Thu, 8-10am recipient's local time
