# Otto Webinar Module 1: "The AI Edge — How Small Businesses Are Quietly Outperforming Giants"

> **Format:** 60–90 min live webinar | **Host:** Otto (Otto) | **Aesthetic:** Swiss Cyberpunk — precision meets rebellion

---

## Target Audience

SMB owners & operators (10–50 employees) who are AI-curious but overwhelmed by the noise. They know AI matters but don't know where to start — or they've tried ChatGPT once and thought "cool, now what?"

---

## Webinar Flow

### 1. THE HOOK (0:00–0:10) — 10 min

**Goal:** Pattern-interrupt. Make them feel seen.

**Talking Points:**
- "You've been told AI will replace you. That's wrong. AI will replace the businesses that *ignore* it."
- Share a concrete stat: SMBs using AI report 40% reduction in operational overhead (McKinsey 2025)
- Quick story: A 12-person marketing agency that cut 20 hours/week of manual work in 30 days
- "This isn't a tech talk. This is a survival briefing."

**Slide Suggestions:**
- Slide 1: Bold title card — dark background, neon accent typography (Swiss Cyberpunk)
- Slide 2: "The AI Gap" — visual showing enterprise adoption vs SMB adoption diverging
- Slide 3: The 12-person agency case study — before/after metrics

---

### 2. THE PROBLEM (0:10–0:25) — 15 min

**Goal:** Articulate their pain better than they can.

**Talking Points:**
- The 3 traps SMBs fall into:
  1. **The Tool Trap** — Buying 10 AI subscriptions, using none properly
  2. **The Hype Trap** — Waiting for the "right" AI, paralyzed by choice
  3. **The DIY Trap** — Having the intern "figure out ChatGPT"
- "You don't have an AI problem. You have a *process* problem that AI can solve."
- Real numbers: Average SMB wastes €2,400/month on unused or misused SaaS tools
- The hidden cost of *not* automating: 15–25 hours/week of manual work per team member on repetitive tasks

**Slide Suggestions:**
- Slide 4: "The 3 Traps" — clean grid layout, icon per trap
- Slide 5: Cost visualization — burning money animation or stark number comparison
- Slide 6: "What your team actually spends time on" — pie chart of manual vs value-add work

---

### 3. THE FRAMEWORK (0:25–0:45) — 20 min

**Goal:** Give them a mental model they can use immediately.

**The Otto A.I.R. Framework:**

> **A**udit → **I**mplement → **R**efine

#### A — Audit (Find the Gold)
- Map every repeating process in the business (takes 2 hours)
- Score each by: frequency × time-per-instance × error-rate
- Top 3 scorers = your AI starting points
- "You don't automate everything. You automate the *right* things."

#### I — Implement (Build the Machine)
- Start with ONE workflow, not ten
- Use existing tools first (most businesses underuse what they already pay for)
- The 80/20 rule: 80% of value comes from automating intake, follow-up, and reporting
- Examples per industry:
  - **Retail:** Automated inventory alerts + customer follow-up sequences
  - **Services:** AI-assisted proposal generation + scheduling
  - **Agencies:** Content brief generation + client reporting

#### R — Refine (Compound the Gains)
- Weekly 15-min review: What broke? What surprised us? What's next?
- Stack automations gradually — each one builds on the last
- Track hours saved, not just "cool factor"

**Slide Suggestions:**
- Slide 7: A.I.R. Framework — clean 3-step visual with icons
- Slide 8–10: One slide per step with key bullets
- Slide 11: Industry examples grid — 3 columns showing before/after per vertical
- Slide 12: "The Compound Effect" — graph showing cumulative hours saved over 6 months

---

### 4. LIVE DEMO (0:45–1:05) — 20 min

**Goal:** Make it real. Show, don't tell.

**Demo Option A — "The 5-Minute Client Onboarding Bot"**
- Build a simple intake automation live using a no-code tool (Make/Zapier + AI)
- Scenario: New client fills out a form → AI summarizes requirements → Creates project brief → Sends to team lead
- Show the before (manual email chain) vs after (60 seconds, zero effort)

**Demo Option B — "AI-Powered Weekly Report"**
- Connect a sample data source (Google Sheets with sales data)
- AI generates a narrative weekly summary with insights
- "Your Monday morning report, written by AI, ready before your coffee"

**Demo Option C — "Smart Customer Follow-Up"**
- Show how an AI agent reads a customer inquiry, categorizes urgency, drafts a personalized response, and flags it for human review
- Emphasize the human-in-the-loop: "AI drafts, you approve"

**Live Demo Tips:**
- Have a backup recording in case of tech issues
- Use real (anonymized) data, not fake examples
- Pause after each step — let it sink in
- Invite audience to type questions in chat during the demo

**Slide Suggestions:**
- Slide 13: "Let's Build Something" — screen transition slide
- Demo runs on screen share — no slides needed
- Slide 14 (post-demo): Recap — what we built, time saved, cost saved

---

### 5. Q&A BUFFER (1:05–1:15) — 10 min

- Take 3–5 live questions
- Prepare planted questions for common objections:
  - "Is this safe for client data?" → Yes, discuss data policies briefly
  - "What if my team resists?" → Change management in 1 sentence
  - "How much does this cost?" → Bridge to CTA

---

### 6. THE CTA (1:15–1:25) — 10 min

**Goal:** One clear next step. No confusion.

**Talking Points:**
- "Everything I showed you today? That's the easy stuff. The real magic is when we map this to *your* business."
- Introduce the **Free AI Audit Call** (30 min, no obligation):
  - We map your top 3 automatable processes
  - You get a custom prioritization scorecard
  - You walk away with a clear 90-day roadmap — whether you work with us or not
- "This isn't a sales call. It's a strategy session. If we're a fit, great. If not, you still leave with a plan."
- Show the booking link — make it dead simple (Calendly or Cal.com)
- Limited slots: "I take 10 audit calls per month. First come, first served."

**Slide Suggestions:**
- Slide 15: "Your Next Step" — single CTA with booking link, large and clear
- Slide 16: What you get from the audit — 3 bullet visual
- Slide 17: Thank you + contact info + social links

## Production Notes

### Visual Design (Swiss Cyberpunk)
- **Colors:** Deep black/charcoal backgrounds, electric blue and neon green accents, white typography
- **Fonts:** Mono/geometric sans-serif (Space Grotesk, JetBrains Mono for code/data)
- **Style:** Minimal slides, lots of whitespace, data-forward, no clip art
- **Motion:** Subtle glitch transitions between sections, clean fade-ins for data

### Tech Setup
- Streaming: Zoom Webinar or StreamYard
- Slides: Figma or Keynote (export to PDF as backup)
- Demo: Pre-configured workspace, tested 1 hour before
- Recording: Always record — repurpose into 5+ content pieces

### Follow-Up Sequence (Post-Webinar)
1. **Immediately:** Thank you email + replay link + audit booking link
2. **Day 2:** "Here's the A.I.R. Framework as a PDF" (lead magnet)
3. **Day 5:** Case study email — deeper dive into the demo example
4. **Day 10:** "Last chance to book your February audit slot"
