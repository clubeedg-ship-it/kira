# ZenithCred — Pilot Target Companies (Netherlands/Belgium)
**Prepared by Kira | February 2026**

---

## Selection Criteria
- 200+ employees (enough traffic for engagement data)
- Active wellness budget or existing programs
- Physical office space (not fully remote)
- Innovation-friendly culture
- Located in Randstad or Flanders for logistics

---

## Tier 1: High-Fit Targets (approach first)

### 1. Booking.com (Amsterdam)
- **Why:** 5,000+ NL employees, known for innovative office culture, large open spaces ideal for interactive floor projectors. Already invests heavily in employee wellbeing.
- **Entry:** HR Innovation / Workplace Experience team
- **Angle:** "Gamify your return-to-office strategy"

### 2. Adyen (Amsterdam)
- **Why:** ~1,500 NL employees, tech-forward, competitive employer needing differentiation. New HQ with modern layout.
- **Entry:** People & Culture team
- **Angle:** "Interactive wellness breaks that reduce burnout in high-pressure fintech"

### 3. ASML (Veldhoven/Eindhoven)
- **Why:** 20,000+ NL employees, massive wellness budget, engineering culture appreciates data-driven solutions. Multiple buildings.
- **Entry:** Corporate Wellness / HSE department
- **Angle:** "Measurable wellness ROI with biofeedback data your engineers will actually trust"

### 4. ING (Amsterdam)
- **Why:** 15,000+ NL employees, agile transformation culture, multiple office floors, existing wellness programs.
- **Entry:** HR Transformation / Workplace Services
- **Angle:** "Gamification tokens for wellness — the same engagement mechanics that work in banking apps"

### 5. Philips (Eindhoven/Amsterdam)
- **Why:** Health tech company — natural alignment. 10,000+ NL employees. Would validate the product's health credibility.
- **Entry:** Health & Wellbeing program lead
- **Angle:** "Health tech for your own people — practice what you preach"

## Approach Strategy
1. **LinkedIn outreach** to HR Innovation / Workplace Experience leads
2. **Email** with one-pager + 30-second video demo
3. **Offer:** Free 2-week trial installation in one common area
4. **Ask:** 15-minute call to discuss pilot setup

## Key Selling Points for All
- Hardware moat (competitors are software-only)
- Biofeedback data = objective wellness metrics (not self-reported surveys)
- Gamification tokens create sustained engagement (not one-off programs)
- ROI measurable within 4-6 weeks of pilot
- Born in the Netherlands — local support, no import complexity
