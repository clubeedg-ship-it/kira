## What Is InterActiveMove?

Interactive floor and wall projection systems that respond to children's movement in real time. No screens, no tablets — just pure physical play combined with educational content. Children run, jump, and interact while the system tracks their motion and adapts instantly.

**200+ games** spanning motor skills, cognitive development, language, math, and social play — with new content added regularly.
