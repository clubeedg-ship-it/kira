# InterActiveMove — 2-Minute Demo Video Script

**Total runtime: ~2:00**

---

## HOOK (0:00–0:05)

**VISUAL:** Close-up of children's feet landing on a colorful projected floor game. Light explodes around their steps. Laughter fills the audio.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "What if your floor could make children move, learn, and laugh — all at the same time?"

---

## PROBLEM (0:05–0:20)

**VISUAL:** Montage: children sitting with tablets, a cluttered toy room, a staff member looking overwhelmed prepping activities, rain on a window (indoor day).

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Screen time is rising. Children are moving less. Staff spend hours setting up activities that lose their magic in weeks. And on rainy days? Every childcare center feels it. You need something that keeps every child active — without adding to your team's workload."

---

## SOLUTION DEMO (0:20–1:20)

**VISUAL:** Wide reveal shot — a bright room with a large interactive floor projection. Children run in and immediately start playing.

**VOICEOVER (0:20–0:30):**
> "Meet InterActiveMove. We project interactive games directly onto your floor or wall. Children step, jump, and move — and the system responds instantly."

**VISUAL (0:30–0:45):** Quick cuts showing different game types:
- Motor skills game: kids jumping between colored zones
- Cognitive game: counting/matching objects by stepping on them
- Collaborative game: two children working together to guide a character
- Sensory/calm game: gentle ripple effects following movement

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Over 200 games — from high-energy obstacle courses to calm sensory experiences. Motor skills, math, language, teamwork. All designed for ages 0 to 12."

**VISUAL (0:45–0:55):** Installation sequence (time-lapse): technician mounting projector on ceiling, calibrating, powering on. Clock shows "under 2 hours."

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Installation takes less than two hours. Ceiling-mounted, out of reach, no renovation needed. One button to start — your staff are ready in minutes."

**VISUAL (0:55–1:05):** Staff member tapping a simple touchscreen menu, selecting a game. Children playing independently while staff observe from the side, smiling.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Staff choose games by age, theme, or learning goal. Or let children explore freely. The system guides the play — so your team can breathe."

**VISUAL (1:05–1:20):** Split screen: (left) a worn-out toy corner vs. (right) the same space with IAM projection active. Then: parents watching during a tour, visibly impressed.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "No toys to replace. No cleanup. No boredom. And parents? They see it and they're sold. It's the wow-factor that makes your location unforgettable."

---

## BENEFITS (1:20–1:40)

**VISUAL:** Clean animated graphics/text overlays appearing one by one, each with a supporting clip:

- **"40% more physical activity"** → kids jumping
- **"Saves staff 2–4 hours/week"** → relaxed team
- **"Pays for itself with 1 extra enrollment"** → parent signing up
- **"Inclusive for all abilities"** → child in wheelchair interacting with projection
- **"New games added automatically"** → notification animation on screen

**VOICEOVER:**
> "40% more movement. Hours saved for your team each week. An investment that pays for itself with just one extra enrollment per year. Inclusive for every child. And the content never gets old — new games are added automatically."

---

**VISUAL:** Children playing joyfully, then freeze-frame. Logo and contact info fade in over a clean white overlay.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Try InterActiveMove free for two weeks. We bring the full system to your location — no cost, no contract, no risk. Let the children decide."

```
🌐 interactivemove.nl

```

**FINAL FRAME (1:50–2:00):** Logo with tagline.

**VOICEOVER:**
> "InterActiveMove. Play that moves with them."

---

## Production Notes

- **Tone:** Warm, energetic, professional. Not corporate — human.
- **Music:** Upbeat, playful instrumental. Drops to gentle during sensory game clip. Builds for CTA.
- **Color grading:** Bright, warm, saturated — matches the vibrancy of the projections.
- **Talent:** Real children in a real kindergarten setting (get parental consent). Real staff, not actors.
- **Languages:** Primary version in Dutch. English subtitle/dub version for international prospects.
- **Aspect ratios:** 16:9 (YouTube/website), 9:16 crop (Instagram/TikTok), 1:1 (LinkedIn/social).
