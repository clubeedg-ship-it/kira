## 2. ABOUT PAGE

### Our Story

**Built on craftsmanship. Driven by results.**

CuttingEdge was founded with a simple belief: great spaces shouldn't require great stress. Too many homeowners and business owners dread renovations — the delays, the miscommunication, the budget overruns. We started CuttingEdge to change that.

We combine creative interior design with disciplined project management. That means you get spaces that look stunning *and* projects that run smoothly. No finger-pointing between designers and builders. No surprises on your invoice. Just a team that owns the process from start to finish.

Based in the Netherlands, we work with homeowners, small businesses, and property investors who want quality without compromise.

### Our Values

**Transparency**
You'll always know where your project stands — budget, timeline, next steps. We over-communicate on purpose.

**Quality Over Speed**
We'd rather take an extra day to get it right than rush through and cut corners. Every detail matters.

**Accountability**
One team, one point of contact. When something needs fixing, we fix it. No excuses, no redirects.

**Respect for Your Space**
Your home is your home. We work clean, communicate schedules clearly, and minimize disruption to your daily life.

### The Team

CuttingEdge brings together experienced interior designers and project managers who share one goal: delivering spaces you love, on time and on budget. Our network of trusted contractors, suppliers, and specialists has been built over years of hands-on work across the Netherlands.

## 3. SERVICES PAGE

### Interior Design

**Spaces that reflect who you are.**

Whether you're redesigning a single room or planning a complete interior, we start by understanding how you live and work. Our design process is collaborative — your input shapes every decision.

**What's included:**
- Initial consultation and space assessment
- Concept development and mood boards
- Floor plans and 3D visualizations
- Material, colour, and furniture selection
- Lighting design
- Supplier sourcing and ordering
- Styling and finishing touches

**Ideal for:** Homeowners renovating or redecorating, new-build interiors, home offices, rental property upgrades.

---

### Project Management

**Your project, professionally managed.**

Renovation projects involve dozens of moving parts — permits, contractors, materials, inspections, timelines. We take that complexity off your plate. As your project manager, we coordinate everything and keep you informed at every stage.

**What's included:**
- Detailed project planning and scheduling
- Contractor selection and coordination
- Budget management and cost tracking
- Permit applications and compliance
- Quality inspections at every milestone
- Regular progress updates
- Problem-solving and risk management

**Ideal for:** Homeowners managing renovations, property investors, small businesses refitting their spaces.

---

### Renovation Coordination

**From demolition to decoration — one team, one plan.**

Full renovations are where our combined design and management expertise really shines. We handle the entire process: designing your new space, planning the build, coordinating all trades, and delivering a finished result you can walk into and love.

**What's included:**
- Complete renovation planning (design + build)
- Kitchen and bathroom renovations
- Structural modifications and rebuilds
- Extension and loft conversion coordination
- Office and retail fit-outs
- Contractor and supplier management
- Final inspection and handover

**Ideal for:** Complete home renovations, kitchen/bathroom remodels, commercial space transformations, property flips.

### How We Work

1. **Free Consultation** — We visit your space, listen to your ideas, and discuss your budget and timeline.
2. **Proposal** — You receive a clear plan with scope, costs, and schedule. No hidden fees.
3. **Design** — We develop the concept with your input at every step.
