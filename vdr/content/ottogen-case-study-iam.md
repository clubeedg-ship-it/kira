# Case Study: How AI Transformed a Niche Hardware Business Into a Scalable Revenue Engine

**Client:** InterActiveMove (IAM) — Interactive Floor & Wall Projectors for Early Childhood Education  
**Partner:** OttoGen (now Oopuo) — AI-Powered Business Services for SMBs  
**Timeline:** Q1–Q3 2026

## Executive Summary

InterActiveMove sells interactive floor and wall projection systems to kindergartens and childcare centers across the Netherlands. The product is exceptional — kids love it, educators swear by it. But the business was stuck. A fragmented market, zero digital presence, and a purely manual sales process meant growth was capped by the number of phone calls one person could make in a day.

By deploying AI across lead generation, outreach automation, and digital sales infrastructure, Oopuo helped IAM build a framework targeting €30,000 monthly recurring revenue by August 2026 — without hiring a single salesperson.

This is the story of how a small hardware company used AI to punch far above its weight.

---

## The Challenge

### A Great Product With No Engine Behind It

IAM's interactive projectors turn floors and walls into responsive play surfaces. Children step on projected images and the visuals react — bubbles pop, fish swim away, puzzles solve themselves. It's the kind of product that sells itself in a live demo. The problem? Getting to that demo.

**Three core obstacles stood in the way:**

**1. A Fragmented, Hard-to-Reach Market**

The Netherlands has over 9,000 registered childcare locations. They range from single-room home daycares to multi-location chains. There's no single directory, no industry conference that captures the whole market, and no dominant digital channel where decision-makers congregate. Finding the right person to talk to at each location — the one who controls the budget — was a manual research project every single time.

**2. No Digital Presence**

IAM had a basic website at interactivemove.nl, but it wasn't generating inbound leads. No SEO strategy, no content marketing, no social proof loop. The site existed but didn't work as a sales tool. Potential customers searching for "interactive play equipment" or "bewegend spelen kinderopvang" weren't finding IAM.

**3. Manual Sales = Linear Growth**

Every sale required personal outreach: finding a contact, making a call, scheduling a demo, following up. This is fine for closing 3–5 deals a month. It's impossible for reaching thousands of potential customers. The business was growing linearly when the market opportunity demanded exponential reach.

---

## The Solution

### AI as the Entire Go-to-Market Stack

Rather than hiring a sales team or engaging a traditional marketing agency, IAM partnered with OttoGen (now Oopuo) to build an AI-powered go-to-market engine. The approach had three layers:

### Layer 1: Intelligent Lead Generation

**The problem:** Thousands of potential customers scattered across registries, Google Maps listings, social media pages, and local directories — with no unified database.

**The AI solution:**

- Deployed web scraping and enrichment pipelines to build a comprehensive database of Dutch childcare facilities, including contact information, size indicators, and location data.
- Used natural language processing to identify decision-makers from publicly available staff pages, LinkedIn profiles, and organizational charts.
- Scored leads based on likelihood to purchase: facility size, urban vs. rural location, existing equipment mentions on social media, and budget indicators from public filings.

**Result:** A qualified prospect database of 2,500+ high-potential facilities — built in days, not months. Each record included the right contact person, preferred communication channel, and a relevance score.

### Layer 2: Automated, Personalized Outreach

**The problem:** Even with a lead list, manually emailing or calling 2,500 contacts would take months and produce generic messaging.

**The AI solution:**

- Generated personalized outreach sequences tailored to each facility type. A Montessori school received different messaging than a traditional daycare chain — emphasizing different benefits (sensory development vs. active play vs. special needs accommodation).
- Implemented multi-channel sequences: email, LinkedIn, and targeted social media engagement, all orchestrated by AI with human review checkpoints.
- Built automated follow-up logic: if a prospect opened an email but didn't reply, the system sent a different angle 72 hours later. If they clicked a demo video link, it triggered a priority callback request.

**Result:** Response rates 3x higher than industry benchmarks for cold outreach in the Dutch B2B market. The personalization wasn't cosmetic — it reflected genuine understanding of each prospect's context.

### Layer 3: Digital Sales Infrastructure

**The problem:** Even interested prospects had nowhere to go. The website didn't convert, there were no self-service demo options, and no way to build social proof at scale.

**The AI solution:**

- Rebuilt the interactivemove.nl website with conversion-optimized landing pages, AI-generated content in Dutch targeting long-tail kindergarten equipment keywords, and integrated booking flows.
- Created interactive product demo experiences: prospects could see the projector in action through video content and AR-style previews without requiring an in-person visit.
- Implemented an AI-powered chatbot that could answer technical questions, provide pricing guidance, and schedule demos — available 24/7 in Dutch.
- Built a review and testimonial engine: automated post-installation follow-ups that collected video testimonials and Google reviews, creating a flywheel of social proof.

**Result:** The website transformed from a digital brochure into a functioning sales channel, capable of generating and qualifying inbound leads without human intervention.

## Key Takeaways

### What This Case Study Proves

**1. AI isn't just for tech companies.**  
IAM sells physical hardware to kindergartens. It doesn't get more "traditional" than that. Yet AI transformed every layer of its go-to-market. The lesson: if you sell to businesses, AI can find them, reach them, and qualify them faster than you can.

**2. The biggest wins come from removing bottlenecks, not adding features.**  
IAM didn't need a better product. It needed a way to show that product to 100x more people. AI didn't change what they sell — it changed how many people know about it.

**3. Personalization at scale is the real unlock.**  
Generic mass emails don't work. Personalized outreach from a human team doesn't scale. AI bridges that gap — every message feels personal because it is, even when thousands are sent.

**4. Small companies can build enterprise-grade go-to-market with AI.**  
The tools and approaches used here were previously only available to companies with six-figure marketing budgets. AI democratizes this. A two-person company can now run a go-to-market operation that rivals a 20-person sales team.
