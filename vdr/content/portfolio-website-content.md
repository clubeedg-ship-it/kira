## 2. ABOUT

### The Builder

Otto started with nothing conventional — no degree, no network, no safety net. Moved to the Netherlands alone at 18. Was homeless twice. Learned from a 75-year-old alchemist, studied neurofeedback, taught himself AI and markets.

At 20, he runs Oopuo.

This isn't a startup story about privilege meeting opportunity. It's about pattern recognition under pressure and the refusal to stay where the world puts you.

### Mission

**Democratize the infrastructure layer.**

Most businesses are locked out of the systems that create real wealth — data pipelines, financial products, AI that actually works. Oopuo builds companies that give SMBs access to these tools, then connects the data into something larger than any single company could be.

### Vision

**A tokenized impact economy by 2030.**

The endgame isn't a portfolio of companies. It's a network where service data flows into financial products, financial products fund impact, and impact is measurable, tradeable, and transparent.

Service → Data → Finance → Impact. Each step funds the next.

### The Thesis

```
Service company
  → data advantage
    → embedded finance
      → tokenized impact economy
```

Every Oopuo company occupies a position in this chain. None exist in isolation.
