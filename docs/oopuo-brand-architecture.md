## 1. THE MODEL: Endorsed Brand Architecture

Oopuo uses an **Endorsed Brand** model (think: Marriott → Courtyard by Marriott, or Alphabet → Google).

The parent brand **Oopuo** gives credibility and infrastructure. Sub-brands serve specific markets but carry the Oopuo endorsement when it helps — and stand alone when it doesn't.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    OOPUO B.V.                       │
│            (Holding / Parent Entity)                │
│       "Privacy-First AI Infrastructure"             │
│                                                     │
│  ┌─────────────┐  ┌─────────────┐  ┌────────────┐  │
│  │  SERVICES   │  │  VENTURES   │  │    CORE    │  │
│  │  (Revenue)  │  │  (Startups) │  │   (R&D)    │  │
│  └──────┬──────┘  └──────┬──────┘  └─────┬──────┘  │
└─────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┼─────────┘
          │                │                │
          ▼                ▼                ▼
    Client Work      Portfolio Co's     Chimera
    (Solyx, MSTA)    (ZenithCred,       Nexus OS
                      SentinAgro,       Kira Platform
                      IAM, etc.)
```

## 2. THE THREE PILLARS

### Pillar 1: OOPUO Services (Client Work → Revenue)

This is the **agency/consultancy arm**. Two tiers based on client sophistication:

#### Tier A — SMB / Growth Clients (e.g. Solyx Energy)
**What they need:** Website, inventory system, customer support, basic automation
**What they see:** A modern, capable AI-powered agency

**Project Fees (one-time):**

| Service | Example (Solyx) | Price |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Web Design & Dev | Brand website, UX, responsive | €3,500 – €8,000 |
| Brand & Strategy | Visual identity, positioning, copy | €2,000 – €5,000 |
| System Setup | Inventory, CRM, integrations | €2,500 – €6,000 |
| AI Support Setup | Chatbot, Vapi voice, email automation | €2,000 – €4,000 |

**Monthly Retainers (separate, stackable):**

| Service | What's Included | Price/mo |
|---------|----------------|----------|
| Website & Maintenance | Hosting, updates, SEO, small changes, uptime monitoring | €397/mo |
| Inventory / Business System | System ops, bug fixes, feature iterations, data backups | €497/mo |
| AI Customer Support | Chatbot/voice ops, training, conversation monitoring, improvements | €497/mo |
| Full Stack (all above) | Everything bundled, priority support, monthly strategy call | €1,097/mo |

**Brand presentation:** "Oopuo" directly. Clean, modern, accessible.
Website: **oopuo.com/services** or **services.oopuo.com**

**Positioning:** *"We build intelligent business systems. Not just websites — systems that think."*

**Example: Solyx Energy total cost:**
- Website build: €5,000 (one-time)
- Inventory system setup: €4,000 (one-time)
- AI support setup: €3,000 (one-time)
- Monthly: Website €397 + Inventory €497 + AI Support €497 = **€1,391/mo**
- Or bundled Full Stack: **€1,097/mo** (save €294)

### Pillar 2: OOPUO Ventures (Startups → Equity)

Portfolio companies that Oopuo builds, owns equity in, and operates.

| Venture | Domain | Oopuo's Role | Equity |
|---------|--------|-------------|--------|
| **ZenithCred** | Corporate wellness gamification | Builder + operator | Majority |
| **SentinAgro** | Drone cattle monitoring | Builder + IP holder | Majority |
| **IAM (Interactive Move)** | Interactive projectors for kids/offices | Operator | Full ownership |
| **CuttingEdge** | Interior design project mgmt | Service partner | Revenue share |
| **Abura Cosmetics** | Sales support | Service partner | Revenue share |

**How it works:**
- Oopuo provides the tech stack, AI, and operational backbone
- Each venture has its own brand identity (no "Oopuo" in their name)
- But the pitch deck says: *"Built on Oopuo infrastructure"*
- Investors fund the venture, but Oopuo retains equity + ongoing tech fees

**For NEW startups you build:**
1. Idea → validate under Oopuo Labs (internal)
2. MVP → launch as independent brand
3. Traction → spin out, Oopuo keeps equity + tech contract
4. Funding → venture raises independently, Oopuo = founding technical partner

## 3. HOW CLIENTS EXPERIENCE IT

### Solyx Energy walks in:
1. Lands on **oopuo.com** → sees clean portfolio, "AI-powered business systems"
2. Discovery call → understands Oopuo builds websites + inventory + support
3. Gets proposal: website (€3K project), inventory system + AI support (€297/mo retainer)
4. Sees a modern agency that happens to be really good at AI
5. **Never hears about Chimera, ZenithCred, or enterprise stuff**

### MSTA walks in:
1. Referred or lands on **oopuo.com/enterprise** → sees case studies, privacy credentials
2. Discovery call → Oopuo demonstrates local AI deployment, governance frameworks
3. Gets proposal: strategy engagement (€5K/mo), local AI deployment (€25K project), ongoing ops (€3K/mo)
4. Sees a serious infrastructure company, not a web agency
5. **Learns about Chimera as proof of deep technical capability**

### An investor evaluates Oopuo:
1. Sees a holding with three pillars: revenue (services), growth (ventures), moat (core)
2. Services = cashflow machine funding everything
3. Ventures = portfolio of high-upside bets
4. Core = defensible IP that makes the whole thing work
5. **This is a platform company, not an agency**

- Footer: *"An Oopuo company"* or *"Built on Oopuo infrastructure"*
- Once Oopuo has brand equity, the endorsement adds value

## 5. LEGAL STRUCTURE (Recommended)

```
Oopuo B.V. (Netherlands)
├── Oopuo Services — revenue from client work
├── Oopuo Ventures B.V. — holds equity in portfolio companies
│   ├── ZenithCred B.V.
│   ├── SentinAgro B.V.
│   └── [Future ventures]
└── Oopuo Core — R&D, IP ownership (Chimera, patents)
```

**Why B.V.?**
- You're in the Netherlands
- Low setup cost (~€1-2K)
- EU jurisdiction (credibility for MSTA-type clients)
- Easy to spin out ventures as separate B.V.s later
- Holding structure = tax efficiency on dividends between entities

**Start with:** Single Oopuo B.V. doing everything. Split into holding + subs when revenue justifies the admin cost (probably at €100K+ annual revenue).
